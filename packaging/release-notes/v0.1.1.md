# FLXXX3 v0.1.1 – Standalone DJ App for Android and the DDJ-FLX4

Bug-fix and feature update after the first tests. Thanks to everyone who tried v0.1.0 and reported back.

## Fixes
- **Crash at start on Android 10–14 fixed**: v0.1.0 declared Android 10 but needed Android 14 or newer (it crashed right after the start, e.g. on the Xiaomi Pad 5). The app is now really built for Android 10 and newer.
- **Crash right after the first start** (Android graphics system, mostly after installing) fixed
- **LOAD** always loads the selected track (before, it sometimes did nothing, or loaded the previous track after fast scrolling); the library closes after every load
- **Track start**: a loaded track waits on the first beat of the grid; PLAY no longer jumps into the phase of the other deck (only with SYNC on, like the Pioneer players)
- **Channel faders** follow a mixer curve (about -16 dB at half way instead of -6 dB)
- **Hotcue colours** match Rekordbox (blue and light blue were the same)
- **Matching keys** also work for Rekordbox keys with two names (e.g. "D#m/Ebm")
- Key shift shows whole semitones after RESET

## New
- **Set recording**: UTILITY > RECORDING, WAV or MP3 (320 kbit/s) of the master output, saved as `Music/FLXXX3/FLXXX3_Record_<date>_<time>`
- **KEY SHIFT** from -12 to +12 semitones
- **Reverb** shows its amount in percent on the Beat FX button
- **Side windows** (FILTER, BEAT FX, JOGWHEEL, BRIGHTNESS) open next to their button, which is highlighted and closes them again; a touch beside them does not move the waveform; they close after 10 s (ADVANCED SETTINGS > WINDOW)
- **MATCHING KEYS** (UTILITY): keys in the lists that fit the master deck are green
- **SHARE LOG** (ADVANCED SETTINGS > INFO): send the app log, e.g. when something goes wrong
- **SOURCE from the FLX4**: SHIFT + press BROWSE opens SOURCE, turn to choose, press to open
- Beat FX times exactly on the beat grid (1/16 steps)
- Folders with a Rekordbox export (`PIONEER/rekordbox/export.pdb`) are read like a USB stick
- Defaults: quantize beat value 1, XDJ view; darker CDJ header; play marker arrow at the bottom

## Good to know
- Tested on a Galaxy Tab A11 (SM-X130, Android 16) with the DDJ-FLX4; not yet tested on Android 10–13 devices – feedback welcome
- Needs Android 10 or newer (64-bit ARM) and a USB host port; plug in the DDJ-FLX4 before starting the app
- The APK is debug-signed: allow "install unknown apps" and sideload it. Installing it over v0.1.0 keeps your settings
- If the Rekordbox Android app is installed, disable it while using FLXXX3 (it grabs the controller when it is plugged in)
- No sampler, crates, auto-DJ or streaming; no other controllers yet

## Install
1. Download `FLXXX3-v0.1.1.apk` and open it on the device (or `adb install -r --no-incremental FLXXX3-v0.1.1.apk`)
2. Connect the DDJ-FLX4 and a Rekordbox-exported USB stick (a powered hub is recommended), then start FLXXX3
3. Choose the stick on the SOURCE screen and press OPEN
