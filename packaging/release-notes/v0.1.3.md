# FLXXX3 v0.1.3 – Standalone DJ App for Android, Windows, the DDJ-FLX4 and the DDJ-FLX10

Feature update: support for the **DDJ-FLX10** (four decks, jog screens), a waveform like Rekordbox, better jog and loop control, played tracks in the library and more.

## Update
FLXXX3 v0.1.2 is signed with the same release key: **install v0.1.3 over it, your settings stay.** (Only v0.1.0 / v0.1.1 had to be uninstalled first.)

## New
- **DDJ-FLX10** (Android and Windows): four decks (UTILITY > DECKS), mapping like Rekordbox: pad modes HOT CUE, BEAT LOOP (1/4 to 32 beats) and BEAT JUMP, SHIFT functions (SHIFT+CUE track start, SHIFT+PLAY stutter, SHIFT+4 BEAT reloop, SHIFT+QUANTIZE beat grid, SHIFT+BEAT SYNC master), BEAT SYNC and SLIP as switches with LED, channel level LEDs, Color FX and Beat FX with the small FX display, MASTER like Rekordbox
- **Jog screens of the FLX10** in Rekordbox mode: waveform, beat grid, time, BPM, tempo %, tempo range and key
- **SLIP** (FLX10): the track jumps to where it would be when you let go of the jog or leave a loop; a line in the waveform shows that position
- **Loop adjust** (SHIFT+IN / SHIFT+OUT and jog): the waveform stands still and a line shows the playing position; works with the slowest jog movement
- **Waveform like Rekordbox** (3 bands: blue, orange, brown, white) with layer sizes and an example in ADVANCED SETTINGS
- **Played tracks**: a green dot in the library after a track played for 20 seconds
- **EJECT** next to OPEN on the SOURCE screen (Windows ejects the drive, Android opens the storage settings)
- **Recording folder** (UTILITY > RECORDING > FOLDER)
- **Beat FX**: LOW CUT ECHO, ENIGMA JET, STRETCH, MOBIUS, ROLL, SLIP ROLL; the 14 positions of the FLX10 selector are assignable in ADVANCED SETTINGS
- **Vinyl brake and start** on PLAY, BACK SPIN of the FLX4 that runs out smoothly, settings in the JOGWHEEL window and in ADVANCED SETTINGS
- **Log**: ADVANCED SETTINGS > INFO > SHARE LOG (Android: share menu; Windows: opens the folder) for problem reports

## Changed
- Tempo ranges 6 / 10 / 16 / WIDE like Rekordbox; the tempo follows the fader at once when the range changes
- The first loop of a track no longer stalls the waveform; loops are not taken over when a track loads; start marker of a loop shows at once
- ADVANCED SETTINGS tidied up (BEAT FX, JOGWHEEL)
- AAC is decoded by FFmpeg itself (no libfdk-aac)

## Good to know
- Tested on a Galaxy Tab A11 (Android 16) and on Windows 11 with the DDJ-FLX4 and the DDJ-FLX10; not tested on Android 10–13 devices – feedback welcome
- **Android**: needs Android 10 or newer (64-bit ARM) and a USB host port; plug in the controller before starting the app. If the Rekordbox Android app is installed, disable it while using FLXXX3
- **Windows**: needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Connect the controller directly (not through a USB hub). The installer is not code-signed: Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" – click "More info" and "Run anyway"
- The fine jog position (loop adjust at slow speed) uses the HID reports of the FLX10
- No sampler, crates, auto-DJ or streaming; only the DDJ-FLX4 and the DDJ-FLX10 as controllers

## Install
**Android**: download `FLXXX3-v0.1.3.apk` and open it on the device (allow "install unknown apps"); connect the controller and a Rekordbox-exported USB stick, start FLXXX3, choose the stick on the SOURCE screen and press OPEN.

**Windows**: download `FLXXX3-v0.1.3-windows-setup.exe` and run it (see the SmartScreen note); connect the controller and the stick, start FLXXX3 from the start menu.

The SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Android signing certificate (SHA-256): `bf372727ed667219ab34f877b0082f9726182a73152c4b23dae207eb5d060429`
